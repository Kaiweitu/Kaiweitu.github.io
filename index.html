I am about to join the AI and System Co-design team at Meta as a Research Scientist.

I recently received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau and Prof. Remzi Arpaci-Dusseau, as a member of The ADvanced Systems Laboratory (ADSL). My research focuses on managing heterogeneous storage and memory systems.

Before coming to Wisconsin, I received a BEng in Computer Science from the University of Michigan-Ann Arbor and a BEng in Electrical Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
